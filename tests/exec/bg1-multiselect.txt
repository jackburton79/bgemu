# AREA: AR0602
#
# Multi-selection: replacing the map selection (Select-Actor), adding/
# removing one member without touching the rest (Toggle-Selected - the
# headless equivalent of a shift-click on an avatar or HUD portrait), and
# a drag-select rectangle (Drag-Select - the headless equivalent of a
# real mouse drag) that either replaces or adds to it. Khalid is spawned
# and recruited here (rather than relying on the default party, which in
# BG1 is Ajanti alone) so there are two party members to select between.
Run-Action -,7,-,0,KHALID,-,520,127
Step-Ticks 2
Run-Action KHALID,19,-,0,-,-,0,0
Step-Ticks 2

Assert-SelectedCount 1
Assert-Selected AJANTI,true

Toggle-Selected KHALID
Assert-SelectedCount 2
Assert-Selected AJANTI,true
Assert-Selected KHALID,true

Toggle-Selected KHALID
Assert-SelectedCount 1
Assert-Selected KHALID,false

Select-Actor KHALID
Assert-SelectedCount 1
Assert-Selected AJANTI,false
Assert-Selected KHALID,true

# A rectangle covering both replaces the selection with both; one over
# empty ground clears it (SetSelectedActors() with nothing inside).
Drag-Select 450,80,600,200,false
Assert-SelectedCount 2
Drag-Select 2000,2000,2100,2100,false
Assert-SelectedCount 0
Drag-Select 450,80,600,200,false
Assert-SelectedCount 2

# A plain click on empty ground with both selected moves both - not to
# the identical point (that would leave them fighting over one search-map
# cell, see AreaRoom.cpp's own kSpawnOffsets comment): a drag-select's
# order follows the area's own actor list, so Ajanti (already placed when
# the area loaded) gets the exact point and Khalid (spawned afterwards,
# later in that list) is spread to a nearby one instead.
Click-Area 440,150
Step-Ticks 60
Assert-Position AJANTI,442,149
Assert-Position KHALID,458,161
