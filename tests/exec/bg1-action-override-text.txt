# Khalid/Jaheira dialog's ActionOverride("Khalid",JoinParty())) queues the

Queue-Action JAHEIR,ActionOverride("Khalid",JoinParty())
Step-Ticks 20
Assert-Trigger KHALID,true,InParty(Myself)
Assert-Trigger JAHEIR,false,InParty(Myself)
